# Filters and channels

Look at the table with the wavelength of each filter. Which channel do you put the filter with the shortest wavelength in? Why there?

<!-- hint -->
Think of a rainbow. What colour is the visible light with the shortest wavelength?

<!-- hint -->
Visible light runs from about 400 nm (violet and blue) to 700 nm (red). Find the filter with the shortest wavelength in the table and think about what colour of light it lets through.

<!-- hint -->
A short wavelength (about 400–500 nm) is blue light, a long one (600–700 nm) red. So the filter with the shortest wavelength (B or g) goes into the blue channel, the middle one (V or r) into the green and the longest (R or i) into the red channel. Then the star colours are in the same order as the eye would see them: hot stars are bluish, cooler ones reddish.
