# Bias level and noise

Open one bias frame. With **box statistics**, choose an area of about 100 × 100 pixels in the middle of the frame. What are its mean and standard deviation?

<!-- hint -->
On the box statistics tab, drag a rectangle on the image. The mean and standard deviation are in the table below. Do not choose an area at the edge where the overscan is.

<!-- hint -->
The mean shows the bias level, the standard deviation the read noise. Both are in ADU.

<!-- hint -->
In the AZT-12 data the mean is about 1454 ADU and the standard deviation about 6 ADU. The standard deviation is this camera's **read noise**: how much a pixel value varies randomly even without light.
