# What if there were no flat?

Switch off **"Divide by the flat"** and calibrate again. Look at one calibrated frame: compare the sky background in the centre and in the corners with box statistics. Then switch the flat back on and compare again.

What did you notice? Why is the flat needed if we want to compare the brightness of stars?

<!-- hint -->
Use the same frame and the same boxes both times. Write down the median of each box.

<!-- hint -->
Compare the result with the master flat: does the background pattern without the flat look like the pattern of the flat?

<!-- hint -->
Without the flat, the background is brighter on one side of the image than on the other – the same pattern as in the master flat. A star in a darker part of the image would also look fainter than it really is. Dividing by the flat makes the background more even, so the brightness of stars can be compared.
