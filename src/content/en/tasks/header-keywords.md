# Header keywords

Open the **header** of a light frame. Which keywords tell the frame type, the filter and the exposure time? Write the keywords (not the values).

<!-- hint -->
The keywords are in the left column of the header. Search the header for "type", "filter" and "exp".

<!-- hint -->
The frame type value is one of LIGHT, DARK, FLAT or BIAS. The exposure time is in seconds – about 30 for a light frame.

<!-- hint -->
In the AZT-12 files the frame type is given by `IMAGETYP`, the filter by `FILTER` and the exposure time by `EXPTIME` (in these files `EXPOSURE` has the same value too). Other programs may use other names, such as `FRAMETYP` or `EXPOSURE` – that is why the app has an instrument profile where they are listed.
